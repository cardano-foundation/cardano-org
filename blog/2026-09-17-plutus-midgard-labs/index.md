Plutus moves from IO Labs to Midgard Labs, joining Se7en Labs, Teragone, ICAN Group, and BlockPQR as specialist teams maintaining core Cardano functions. Midgard Labs already audits and writes Plutus smart contracts, contributes to the Plutarch and Aiken ecosystems, and its CEO helped bring Plutus tooling into the Plutus Pioneer Program. The transition is coordinated openly through Intersect.
