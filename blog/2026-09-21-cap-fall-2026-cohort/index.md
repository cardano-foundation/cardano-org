The Cardano Foundation announced eight ventures for the Cardano Accelerator Program Fall 2026 cohort. Five ventures joined the Core track and three the Spotlight track, focusing on digital product passports, supply chain traceability, verified identity, and responsible AI oracles. Each team receives a CHF 10,000 contribution and prepares for a February 2027 Demo Day.
