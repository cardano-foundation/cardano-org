Dijkstra will carry Leios, but the fork only makes the protocol available. Pools then register the BLS keys they vote with, and Leios switches on once they cover roughly 90 to 95% of stake, though parameters still set the exact threshold. Endorser block transactions reach the ledger in about 45 to 60 seconds, and at least two later parameter updates aim for 10 to 15 times the 4.5 TxkB/s ceiling.
