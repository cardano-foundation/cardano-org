The fork makes Leios available but does not raise capacity. Pools register their BLS voting keys after Dijkstra, and Leios switches on once those cover roughly 90 to 95% of delegated stake, a figure parameters still set. Once Leios is active, endorser block transactions are expected to reach the ledger in roughly 45 to 60 seconds, from 3 to 5 times the 4.5 TxkB/s ceiling toward 10 to 15 times with later updates.
