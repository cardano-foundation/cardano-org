Five node implementations, cardano-node, Amaru, Dingo, cardano-bean, and TSUNAGI, have now produced blocks on Cardano mainnet, with Dingo passing 100 mainnet blocks. The Civics Committee approved the 2027 Constitutional Committee election timeline for three seats, while EMURGO postponed its DRep retirements to late December. Intersect and MIGSO-PCUBED also won an APM Innovation in Project Management Award, and Monument Bank launched tokenized sterling deposits on Midnight.
