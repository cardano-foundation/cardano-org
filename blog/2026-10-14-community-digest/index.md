The Cardano Foundation co-organized a Rooftop UTXO Pitch Night with Draper Dragon, putting up to $500,000 in front of 90+ investors and nine teams. On mainnet, CIP-0113 lets issuers embed identity, sanctions, and freeze rules inside the asset itself, enforced by the ledger on every transfer. A draft constitutional amendment covering the new hard fork parameters also sits open for review ahead of the Dijkstra vote.
