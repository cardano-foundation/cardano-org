Valentine was an intra-era hard fork of Babbage, enacted at epoch 394 on February 14, 2023. It moved the protocol version to 8.0 and focused on Plutus and performance follow-ups after [Vasil](/glossary/vasil), rather than opening a new ledger era. See the [hard forks](/hardforks/) timeline for the surrounding upgrades.
