EMURGO is the commercial pioneer entity of Cardano, working on ventures, enterprise adoption, and products that use the chain. Together with the [Cardano Foundation](/glossary/cardano-foundation) and [Input Output](/glossary/input-output) it formed the original launch triad. Protocol control now sits with on-chain governance rather than with any pioneer entity.
