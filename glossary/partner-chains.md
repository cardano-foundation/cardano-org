Partner chains are app-specific or feature-specific blockchains that connect to Cardano instead of living as a Cardano ledger era. They typically keep their own consensus and virtual machine while using Cardano for settlement, bridging, or shared security. [Midnight](/glossary/midnight) is one example; [Hydra](/glossary/hydra) heads are a related but distinct scaling approach based on state channels rather than a separate L1.
