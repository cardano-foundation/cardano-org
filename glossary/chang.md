The Cardano hard fork that activated the Conway ledger era on September 1, 2024, switching on the first batch of on-chain governance features from CIP-1694: protocol parameter changes, hard-fork-initiation, and Info governance actions.

Later Conway-era hard forks built on that foundation. [Plomin](/glossary/plomin) (January 2025) enabled the remaining governance actions such as treasury withdrawals and constitution updates, and [van Rossem](/glossary/van-rossem) (July 2026) was the first hard fork proposed and ratified entirely through the on-chain process Chang introduced. The next one, [Dijkstra](/glossary/dijkstra), moves Cardano into a new ledger era.
