Input Output (often styled IOG, formerly IOHK) is the research and engineering organization that designed Ouroboros and continues to deliver large parts of the Cardano node, ledger, and Plutus tooling. It is one of the three pioneer entities alongside the [Cardano Foundation](/glossary/cardano-foundation) and [EMURGO](/glossary/emurgo). It does not unilaterally control the chain: hard forks and parameter changes are ratified by DReps, stake pool operators, and the Constitutional Committee.
