A Constitutional Amendment Proposal proposes exact changes to the text of the [Cardano Constitution](/glossary/constitution). Each proposal sets out a summary, the motivation, an analysis of the change, and its expected impact. It then goes through a public deliberation period on the [Constitutional Amendment Portal](https://cap.intersectmbo.org/), which Intersect opened for alpha testing in August 2026. Authors keep ownership of their proposals; editors facilitate the process but do not control it.

A proposal accepted through this process does not change the constitution by itself. The change takes effect only when a constitution-update [governance action](/glossary/governance-action) is ratified by DReps and approved by the [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee). Using the portal is optional, and anyone can submit that governance action directly. To describe a problem without proposing new wording, authors use a [Constitutional Issue Statement](/glossary/constitutional-issue-statement) instead.
