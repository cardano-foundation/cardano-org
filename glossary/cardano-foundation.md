The Cardano Foundation is an independent non-profit based in Switzerland and one of the three pioneer entities (with [Input Output](/glossary/input-output) and [EMURGO](/glossary/emurgo)) that launched Cardano. It focuses on adoption, education, standards, and ecosystem programmes. Since 2026 it has also stewarded [Project Catalyst](/glossary/project-catalyst). Like the other pioneer entities, it no longer controls genesis keys; protocol changes go through on-chain governance.
