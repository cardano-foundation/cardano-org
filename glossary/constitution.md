The Cardano Constitution sets out the principles that governance actions must respect, the guardrails for protocol parameters and treasury spending, and the roles of DReps, stake pool operators and the [Constitutional Committee](/glossary/constitutional-committee). It was drafted in community workshops and finalized at a constitutional convention in Buenos Aires and Nairobi in December 2024, replacing the interim constitution that had applied since the Chang hard fork.

DReps ratified the constitution on-chain in epoch 541, shortly after the [Plomin](/glossary/plomin) hard fork enabled the update-constitution action, and it took effect at the following epoch boundary on February 23, 2025. Part of its guardrails is enforced by the guardrails script, a Plutus script that every parameter change and treasury withdrawal must satisfy.

The constitution can be amended through the same governance action. An [updated version](https://cardano.org/news/2026-01-22-update-cardano-constitution/), ratified with 79 percent of DRep stake, has been in force since epoch 609 (January 24, 2026). It tightened the requirements for off-chain documents and removed budget info actions, so treasury withdrawals must be fully self-contained. Further amendments, for instance to cover the parameters the Dijkstra era introduces, follow the same path. Read the full text at [cardano.org/constitution](/constitution/).
