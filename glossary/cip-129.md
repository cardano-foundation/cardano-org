CIP-129 specifies a single-byte header plus bech32 encoding for Conway governance identifiers so tools can tell DRep, Constitutional Committee, and governance-action ids apart even when stored as raw bytes. Status: Proposed. Explorers and wallets use these forms when linking [governance actions](https://explorer.cardano.org/).
