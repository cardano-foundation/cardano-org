Intersect is a membership organization for the Cardano ecosystem. Its committees and working groups help steward open-source delivery, parameter changes, and community programmes, while protocol changes themselves are ratified on-chain under [CIP-1694](/glossary/cip-1694). Intersect does not hold genesis keys and cannot unilaterally change the ledger; it is one of the institutions that support the governance process alongside the pioneer entities.
