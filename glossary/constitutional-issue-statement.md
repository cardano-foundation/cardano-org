A Constitutional Issue Statement defines a problem in the [Cardano Constitution](/glossary/constitution), such as an ambiguity, a procedural gap, or a governance risk, without proposing new wording. Authors submit one on the [Constitutional Amendment Portal](https://cap.intersectmbo.org/) when they have spotted a problem but are not ready to propose a solution, or when the problem is minor.

An issue statement is useful on its own as a shared record of a governance challenge, and it can lead to one or more [Constitutional Amendment Proposals](/glossary/constitutional-amendment-proposal). A single proposal can also resolve several issue statements. Neither document changes the constitution; that still takes a ratified on-chain [governance action](/glossary/governance-action).
